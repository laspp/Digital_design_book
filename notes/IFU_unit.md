### The IFU CTRL  
    - The goal of IFU ctrl is to manage flow of IFU 
    - Three tasks:
      - First task generate internal signals InstrEnF and PCEnF signals that store instruction and PC 
      - Second task: facilatate memory request to the instruction memory through obi interface, ObiReqF 
      - Third task: notifying ther stages that the IFU finished processing with signal ValidF
    - Why do we need IFU controller
      - fetching instructions does not happen in one cycle. The CTRL shoudl watch on the OBI signals (ObiGntF and ObiRvalidF)
      - jump scenario: when jump occurs we need to restart fetching memory and if needed discarded wrongly fetched instruction
      - The IDU stage is not ready. Try to freeze the IFU stage until IDU is ready
